Tokyo Sips – Brews, Bytes, and Bites

Bonjour from Tokyo! I'm a French woman who has been living in Japan for six years, diving into the city's coffee scene. Welcome to Tokyo Sips, where I sip, eat, and code my way through the best spots while learning HTML from scratch — join my journey!

In this section, I’ll take you through some of my favorite hidden gems and popular spots around Tokyo that are still unknown to many foreigners.

But not only that — I will also share small tips to make certain things in Japan a little easier for you. For example: which hospital or clinic to choose, which one to go to for specific needs, and which places you might prefer to avoid — and why. Of course, all of this information is based on my own experiences. Others may have different perspectives, and that’s completely valid.

This website will evolve and change regularly. For now, I am studying HTML on my own at home, before eventually having enough savings to attend school and change my career. I hope you’ll enjoy the content, even if it looks a little messy in the beginning.
