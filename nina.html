Nina's Café A Taste of Versailles in Shinjuku

Nestled in the heart of bustling Shinjuku, Nina's Café is like stepping into a Marie Antoinette-inspired oasis.
With its elegant French décor and a menu that’s a world apart, this café offers a relaxing escape from the city's hustle.
Whether you're sipping on a delicate French tea or enjoying a unique coffee blend, you’ll feel as though you’ve been transported to another world. It’s the perfect spot to unwind and enjoy a little taste of France right here in Tokyo.

I often go to this place not because it matches the cliché most people imagine about Versailles or Paris,
but because it soothes me. Strange, right? And you might ask, why?

The reason is simple. When I was a child, an anime used to air on TV every Saturday. Some of you may know it. Others might be too young, or perhaps it had a different name in your country. In French, it was called La Rose de Versailles.

Every time I watched it, I imagined myself in the place of the heroine, Lady Oscar. She represented a strong woman, standing for her country and fighting not just for herself. In a way, she reminded me of Jeanne d’Arc — with differences, of course. Unlike Jeanne d’Arc, Lady Oscar came from a noble family, as the story tells us.

When I was a child, I often imagined myself in the heroine’s place and asked myself:
would I do the same? Would I react the same way as her?

This café helps me remember the feelings I had as a child while watching that anime — those moments of imagination, strength, and quiet dreaming.
